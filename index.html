Frequently Asked Questions

What is a USDC card?

A USDC card is a crypto debit card that lets you spend USDC stablecoins at any merchant accepting Visa or Mastercard. USDC is pegged 1:1 to the US Dollar, eliminating crypto volatility risk.

Can I spend USDC without selling it first?

Yes! Self-custody cards like MetaMask, Gnosis Pay, and Phantom let you spend directly from your wallet. The card auto-converts to fiat at the point of sale.

Which USDC card has the best cashback?

KAST Founders offers up to 15%, Bybit up to 10%, and Plutus up to 9% in PLU tokens. Ether.fi Cash gives 4.08% in ETHFI with DeFi-backed credit.

Are USDC cards available worldwide?

Many USDC cards like KAST, Redotpay, and Ready support 100+ countries. European users have the most options including Gnosis Pay, Plutus, and Holyheld.
