FAQ

Does Bleap support USDC?

Yes, Bleap supports USDC stablecoin spending. You can load USDC and spend it at any Mastercard merchant worldwide, with automatic conversion to local fiat currency.

What cashback does Bleap offer?

Bleap offers 2% cashback paid in USDC. Rates may vary based on your tier or staking level.

Is Bleap self-custody?

Bleap is non-custodial. You maintain control of your private keys and funds at all times.

What are the fees for Bleap?

Bleap charges 0% in FX fees with an annual fee of $0. The card operates on the Mastercard network.
