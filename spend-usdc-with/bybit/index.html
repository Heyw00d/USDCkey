FAQ

Does Bybit support USDC?

Yes, Bybit supports USDC stablecoin spending. You can load USDC and spend it at any Mastercard merchant worldwide, with automatic conversion to local fiat currency.

What cashback does Bybit offer?

Bybit offers 2-10% cashback paid in USDT. Rates may vary based on your tier or staking level.

Is Bybit self-custody?

Bybit is custodial. Your funds are held by the platform, which manages security on your behalf.

What are the fees for Bybit?

Bybit charges 0.9% in FX fees with an annual fee of €10. The card operates on the Mastercard network.
