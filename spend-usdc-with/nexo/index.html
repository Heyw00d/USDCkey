FAQ

Does Nexo support USDC?

Yes, Nexo supports USDC stablecoin spending. You can load USDC and spend it at any Mastercard merchant worldwide, with automatic conversion to local fiat currency.

What cashback does Nexo offer?

Nexo offers 0.5-2% cashback paid in NEXO. Rates may vary based on your tier or staking level.

Is Nexo self-custody?

Nexo is custodial. Your funds are held by the platform, which manages security on your behalf.

What are the fees for Nexo?

Nexo charges 0-0.5% in FX fees with an annual fee of $0. The card operates on the Mastercard network.
